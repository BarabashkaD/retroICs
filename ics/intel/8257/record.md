# Intel 8257 programmable DMA controller: data sheet record

Data sheet items of the Intel 8257 (КР580ВТ57).

## Sources

Source: [C78] 8257/8257-5 data sheet, printed pp. 12-98..12-110 (PDF 606-618), the only 8257 data sheet in the collection. [UM83] has no 8257 data sheet (the 8257 is only named: PDF pp. 18, 143, 147-148, 165 (appendix A1-23, slave bus parameters of the 8257-5 against the 8085A-2)), so there is no second edition to compare. Times are given as edges of the 8257 clock (θ↑, θ↓) in its states, and in TCY (one clock period) and Tθ (the clock high time). A state runs from one θ↓ to the next: the state separators of the 12-110 waveforms sit on the falling clock edges (renders p618_c13_8_33_20, p618_c44_8_68_22).

## Notation

The original IDs are kept; new functions are numbered after them. The timing sections below ("Transfer states", "HOLD overhead with an 8080A") are cited by their names, not repeated. "rel N" in the model's comments: the clock-enable count of the TB harness from the θ↓ that starts S1 (defined in each TB header). Renders cited by name are page crops of the cited documents, kept with the maintainer's source collection, not in this repository (`<document>_pNN` = PDF page NN; `_cA_B_C_D` = a crop in percent of the page: left, top, right, bottom). Researched 2026-11-10.

Notation: θ = the 8257 CLK, θ↑ and θ↓ its edges; TCY one clock period, Tθ the high time (θ↑ to θ↓). In an 8080 system θ is Φ2 (TTL): θ↑ = Φ2↑, θ↓ = Φ2↓. An 8257 state runs from one θ↓ to the next (the state bars of the [C78] 12-110 waveforms sit on the falling clock edges, and the widths TRWM = 2TCY + Tθ for "Rd↓ at θ↑(S2) to Rd↑ at θ↓(S1)" and TWWME = 2TCY for "θ↑(S2) to θ↑(S4)" in the 12-109 table hold only with that boundary). So θ↑(Sx) comes TCY - Tθ after the θ↓ that starts Sx and Tθ before the θ↓ that ends it.

| DMA-1 | Four channels, fixed priority, burst | Four channels CH-0..CH-3, each with a DRQn input, a DACKn output, a 16-bit DMA address register and a 16-bit terminal count register. With mode bit 4 = 0 the priority is fixed: CH-0 highest, CH-3 lowest. The 8257 serves only enabled channels ("upon receiving a DMA transfer request from an enabled peripheral"). It keeps the bus and repeats transfers while a peripheral holds its request ("burst"): S4 → S1 directly on HRQ · HLDA (Fig. 6), AEN and HRQ stay high, DACK goes high for S1 and low again at θ↓(S2) of the next cycle (waveform "consecutive cycles and burst mode sequence"). DRQ is sampled at θ↓(SI) and at the θ↓ that starts S4 (TQS ≥ 120 ns), so a request sampled at the θ↓ that starts S4 decides whether S1 follows at the θ↓ that ends S4. In every S0 the DRQs are sampled again and the priority is resolved. When the priority for a back-to-back S1 (S4 → S1) is resolved: SILENT (see below). | [C78] 12-99 (PDF 607) "Block Diagram Description", 12-100 (PDF 608) DRQ text, 12-103 (PDF 611), 12-105 (PDF 613) "DMA Operation", 12-109 (PDF 617) TQS, 12-110 (PDF 618) waveforms, Fig. 6 (12-104, PDF 612) |
| DMA-2 | Rotating priority | Mode bit 4 = 1. - DMA-15: the planned model reads the 12-100 / 12-105 text as "HRQ stays high, S4 → S0". Fig. 6 has no S4 → S0 arc, and the 12-110 waveform drops HRQ at θ↑(S4) and passes SI twice before S0. DACK↑ and IOR↑ come from the same edge (TAK ≤ 250 ns, TDCT ≤ 200 ns, no minimums), so the 8275's "WR↑ to DACK↑ ≥ 0" is not guaranteed by the 8257's numbers; a model should keep DACK active at the IOR rising edge.
